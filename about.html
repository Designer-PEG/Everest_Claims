Our Story

Founded in 2024, Everest Claims and Advisory Pvt Ltd emerged from a vision to transform Nepal's insurance claims landscape.

Named after the world's highest peak, we embody the resilience, integrity, and towering standards that Mount Everest represents.

Prior to officially establishing the firm, our founders accumulated years of hands-on expertise in loss surveying and claims adjustment. We established this organization with a singular, resolute focus: to uphold absolute integrity and bring transparency to every claims assessment in Nepal.
